How to doFamily Devotions

From Jimmy and Laura Seibert

“The family that prays together stays together.” This simple phrase really is a truth.

When we were raising our kids, the Lord gave us a vision to build a family of love, honor, and purpose. Family devotions were a key part of creating that environment in our home. For us it was a very simple process that looked something like this:

  1. 01

    Give thanks

    We pray for breakfast, and then everyone describes one thing they are thankful for. “In everything give thanks; for this is God’s will for you in Christ Jesus.” We want to create thankful hearts and thankful attitudes, and there is always something to be thankful for.

  2. 02

    Read and talk

    Then we share a passage of Scripture or a thought from a devotion, and we discuss it for about five minutes.

  3. 03

    Pray

    We usually pray for one missionary, one need, and then over our day.

This simple structure has allowed us to stay connected and to put a stake in the ground: we start every day with God at the center.

Time alone with Jesus, at every stage

Along with family devotions, we wanted to help our children build their own personal devotional life. We knew this would help them walk with Jesus when they were young and would be a habit they could joyfully carry with them into adulthood.

From the day they were born, even while being held in our arms, our kids were with us while we read the Bible, prayed and sought God. We would read the Bible to them and pray for them. As soon as they were old enough to speak, we taught them Scripture, had them memorize it, and helped them begin to understand God.

Each of our kids has time alone with Jesus every morning. When they were little, we started with short Bible story videos. While we spent time with the Lord, they would watch these stories about Jesus, and then we would talk about them together. As they got a little older, we got them large-print kids’ Bibles. They would read, pray, wait on God and learn to hear Him.

At every stage of life we sat side by side with them, helped them get started, and taught them to spend time alone with Jesus. Today, with grown kids who are now raising their own children, it is our joy that they have carried this value with them and are passing it on to our grandkids, not because we ask them to, but because they have found it to be the source of life they need for everything in front of them.

Remember, your example matters in everything.

Don’t Miss Jesus

The Holiday season was always a whirlwind for us, but family devotions helped us meaningfully encounter God together in the midst of all the events of the season. As you go through these 40 days, you can use these devotionals not only for your personal devotional life, but to fuel your family devotions.

We love you, and we are excited to encounter Jesus with you this Holiday season!

Jimmy and Laura
