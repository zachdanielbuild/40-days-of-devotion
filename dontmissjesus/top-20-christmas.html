Christmas

Top 20 ways to have aJoy-Filled Christmas

From Jimmy and Laura Seibert

Ideas for your own time with God, for your family and the people you gather with, and for Christmas Day.

Personal

  1. Start each day of the holidays alone with God. Pray through passages like Psalm 103:1–6 and Isaiah 9:6–7, pausing to be with Jesus as you connect with the One who created you.
  2. Take at least one day to put your phone down. Choose a set time to text or check email, but step away from the constant communication and be present with your family. This season invites us to pause and engage with the love Jesus has for us and our love for one another.
  3. After Christmas, take time to complete the Roles & Goals worksheet.

Family and the people I gather with

  1. Pick a family game to play every day and build on it throughout the holiday. It can be a card game or a board game. Our family finds so much joy and love in doing something fun together.
  2. Join our church family for our Christmas Eve and New Year’s Eve services.
  3. Find someone to give to. Whether it’s an individual or a cause, find someone or something outside your immediate family that you can give to and show Christ’s love to this holiday season.
  4. Watch The Chosen Christmas special.
  5. Have a Christmas cookie decorating or baking contest.
  6. Drive around and look at Christmas lights, and have everyone secretly pick their favorite house. After you get back home, talk about your favorite!
  7. Make or give small Christmas gifts to your mail carrier. This season is busy for them, so look for simple ways to bless them.
  8. Visit a nursing home and sing Christmas carols to the residents as a family or Lifegroup!
  9. Watch The Nativity Story together.
  10. Make a fort and have a family campout in front of the Christmas tree one night. Your back will be sore, but memories will be made!
  11. Pray and ask the Lord to highlight any family members who need special encouragement this season, then reach out to them.

Christmas Day around the tree and the table

  1. Before you open presents on Christmas Day, read the story of Jesus from Luke 2:1–20, sing one Christmas carol together, and pray prayers of gratitude for Jesus.
  2. Read the Christmas story and have family members act out the nativity.
  3. Before each gift is opened, speak encouragement over that person: how God sees them and how they’ve blessed you in 2026.
  4. Share one way each person has been a gift to your life this year.
  5. Sit together and call out the specific “gifts” God has placed in each person, for the whole family to celebrate.
  6. Extra conversation starters:
    • What is one thing you want to do in 2027 that is for the good of someone other than yourself?
    • What quality of God are you most thankful for this Christmas?
    • What is the best Christmas gift you have ever received?
    • Share an encouragement with someone around your table. What is one way they have blessed you or done something meaningful for you in 2026?
    • What attribute of God have you been reminded of by a friend or family member in 2026?
