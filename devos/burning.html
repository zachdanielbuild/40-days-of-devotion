Day 18 of 40 · The Word

Good Soil

Word

“But the seed in the good soil, these are the ones who have heard the word in an honest and good heart, and hold it fast, and bear fruit with perseverance.”

Luke 8:15

Daily Devotional

Luke 8:15 uses the metaphor of seeds planted in good soil that produce fruit over time. This Scripture helps us understand that when we receive God’s Word with an honest and open heart, holding fast to what is true, it produces fruit in us as we abide in Christ and persevere. It reminds us that our heart posture matters as we read and meditate on the Word of God. As we keep a pure heart before Him, His Word bears lasting fruit in our lives.

Scriptures for Today

As you read today's Scriptures, read slowly and attentively, allowing the Holy Spirit to speak to you as you meditate on His Word.

Luke 8:15

“But the seed on good soil stands for those with a noble and good heart, who hear the word, retain it, and by persevering produce a crop.”

Colossians 1:6

“In the same way, the gospel is bearing fruit and growing throughout the whole world—just as it has been doing among you since the day you heard it and truly understood God’s grace.”

Galatians 5:22-23

“But the fruit of the Spirit is love, joy, peace, forbearance, kindness, goodness, faithfulness, gentleness and self-control. Against such things there is no law.”

Worship

Prayer

Lord, would you create in me a good, open and honest heart as I read and meditate on Your Word. I pray my life will bear fruit as I meditate on Your Word. Help me persevere through seasons that are hard or when I don’t feel like spending time with You. Draw close to me today and speak to me through Your written Word.

Listen & Obey

Spend two minutes in silent, listening prayer. Write down what you sense the Holy Spirit speaking to you. Here are some prompts to assist you:

  • Lord, what do You want to speak to me from these passages of Scripture as I start my day?
  • How do You want me to apply this truth today?
  • Who do I need to share this with today?
