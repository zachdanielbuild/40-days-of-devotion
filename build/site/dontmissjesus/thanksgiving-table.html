Thanksgiving

Give thanks atThe Table

From Jimmy and Laura Seibert

“In everything give thanks; for this is God’s will for you in Christ Jesus.” 1 Thessalonians 5:18

Many of us grew up in homes where thanksgiving and appreciation for one another were not voiced very often. A few years ago, my brother and I took some time to honor our parents in front of our children. We talked about the valuable things they had given us. We were able to build them up, and our kids got to see what it looks like to honor your parents along the journey.

There are so many ways to do this at the Thanksgiving table:

  1. 01

    A Scripture at every plate

    Put a verse in front of everyone’s plate. Have each person read their Scripture out loud and say what they are thankful for.

  2. 02

    A name on every tag

    Put someone’s name on each place card, and have each person say one encouraging thing about the person whose name they drew.

  3. 03

    Honor your grandparents

    If grandparents are at the table, take time to honor them, and let the younger ones see what honor and thanksgiving are all about.

  4. 04

    Remember and pray

    Read Abraham Lincoln’s 1863 Thanksgiving Proclamation together. Be in awe again of what this season is all about, and take some time to pray for our country.

More for the day

  • Tell the story. Talk about the history of Thanksgiving.
  • Remember His faithfulness. Ask: What blessing are you thankful for? Where did you find God faithful in a hard time?
  • Have fun. Joy is a partner with thankfulness. Play a family game together.
  • Speak life. Encourage one another, especially the college students and young adults at the table who are learning what it means to be an adult and give life to others.

Give thanks all day long

Set a reminder on your watch or phone for every hour. Each time it goes off, take a moment with 1 Thessalonians 5:18:

  • Acknowledge God’s presence and His love for you.
  • Thank Him for what is happening in that moment.
  • Pray, “Speak, Lord, Your servant is listening.”

Make it a thankfulness feast

Gather the family around the table and let everyone share three things they are thankful for. Make room for deep conversation, and finish with something you do together as a family.

We are sharing these because we want you to have a great Thanksgiving!

Jimmy and Laura
