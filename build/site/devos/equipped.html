Day 21 of 40 · The Word

Sharper Than a Two-Edged Sword

Word

“For the word of God is living and active and sharper than any two-edged sword, and piercing as far as the division of soul and spirit, of both joints and marrow, and able to judge the thoughts and intentions of the heart.”

Hebrews 4:12

Daily Devotional

The Word of God is not something to read passively. It is alive and active, able to bring life transformation because God Himself is alive, and His Word has been revealed through the person of Jesus. Scripture does not return void; it penetrates our souls, working within us, correcting us, and shaping us as we are spiritually formed to become more like Christ. It pierces like a sword—both a weapon we wield against the enemy and a discerner of the innermost parts of our hearts.

Scriptures for Today

As you read today's Scriptures, read slowly and attentively, allowing the Holy Spirit to speak to you as you meditate on His Word.

Ephesians 6:17

And take the helmet of salvation, and the sword of the Spirit, which is the word of God.

Proverbs 2:1-8

My son, if you accept my words and store up my commands within you, turning your ear to wisdom and applying your heart to understanding— indeed, if you call out for insight and cry aloud for understanding, and if you look for it as for silver and search for it as for hidden treasure, then you will understand the fear of the Lord and find the knowledge of God. For the Lord gives wisdom; from his mouth come knowledge and understanding. He holds success in store for the upright, he is a shield to those whose walk is blameless, for he guards the course of the just and protects the way of his faithful ones.

Joshua 1:7-9

Only be strong and very courageous, being careful to do according to all the law that Moses my servant commanded you. Do not turn from it to the right hand or to the left, that you may have good success wherever you go. This Book of the Law shall not depart from your mouth, but you shall meditate on it day and night, so that you may be careful to do according to all that is written in it. For then you will make your way prosperous, and then you will have good success. Have I not commanded you? Be strong and courageous. Do not be frightened, and do not be dismayed, for the Lord your God is with you wherever you go.”

Worship

Prayer

Lord, help me to hide nothing from You, and help me keep Your Words in my heart so that they continuously transform me. Thank You that I can trust Your Word will not return to me void, but is always working in and through me.

Listen & Obey

Spend two minutes in silent, listening prayer. Write down what you sense the Holy Spirit speaking to you. Here are some prompts to assist you:

  • Lord, what do You want to speak to me from these passages of Scripture as I start my day?
  • How do You want me to apply this truth today?
  • Who do I need to share this with today?
