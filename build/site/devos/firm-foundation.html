Day 17 of 40 · The Word

Firm Foundation

Word

“Therefore everyone who hears these words of mine and puts them into practice is like a wise man who built his house on the rock.”

Matthew 7:24

Daily Devotional

God’s Word is not just something that we read and seek to know. His Word is meant to be put into practice. As we do, we build the house of our lives on the timeless foundation of His faithfulness and wisdom. Each day when we read His Word in our devotional times, it readies us to put His Word into practice in our daily lives.

Scriptures for Today

As you read today's Scriptures, read slowly and attentively, allowing the Holy Spirit to speak to you as you meditate on His Word.

Matthew 7:24-28

“Therefore everyone who hears these words of mine and puts them into practice is like a wise man who built his house on the rock. The rain came down, the streams rose, and the winds blew and beat against that house; yet it did not fall, because it had its foundation on the rock. But everyone who hears these words of mine and does not put them into practice is like a foolish man who built his house on sand. The rain came down, the streams rose, and the winds blew and beat against that house, and it fell with a great crash.” When Jesus had finished saying these things, the crowds were amazed at his teaching, 29 because he taught as one who had authority, and not as their teachers of the law.

Worship

Prayer

Lord, Your Word is trustworthy and reliable. As I read Your Word today, I commit by Your grace to put it into practice. Would you help me be like a wise man, building my house on the rock of Your Word and integrating Your truth into my daily walk with You!

Listen & Obey

Spend two minutes in silent, listening prayer. Write down what you sense the Holy Spirit speaking to you. Here are some prompts to assist you:

  • Lord, what do You want to speak to me from these passages of Scripture as I start my day?
  • How do You want me to apply this truth today?
  • Who do I need to share this with today?
