Faith Which First Lived

Word

When the Apostle Paul writes to Timothy about growing as a disciple of Jesus, Paul speaks to him as a spiritual son. He also points to the sincere faith that first lived in Timothy's grandmother Lois and mother Eunice, and now was evident in Timothy's life. Timothy had been invested in by others and his discipleship was the fruit of their investment.

Today as we grow as disciples, let us reflect on with gratitude our own Eunices, Loises and Pauls, who have invested in us to helped us be the disciples of Jesus we are today.

Scriptures for Today

As you read today's Scriptures, read slowly and attentively, allowing the Holy Spirit to speak to you as you meditate on His Word.

2 Timothy 1:1-6

Paul, an apostle of Christ Jesus by the will of God, in keeping with the promise of life that is in Christ Jesus,

To Timothy, my dear son:

Grace, mercy and peace from God the Father and Christ Jesus our Lord.

I thank God, whom I serve, as my ancestors did, with a clear conscience, as night and day I constantly remember you in my prayers. Recalling your tears, I long to see you, so that I may be filled with joy. I am reminded of your sincere faith, which first lived in your grandmother Lois and in your mother Eunice and, I am persuaded, now lives in you also.

For this reason I remind you to fan into flame the gift of God, which is in you through the laying on of my hands.

Worship

Prayer

Jesus, thank You for the spiritual mothers and fathers who have invested in me. Thank You for the faith that first lived in them and now is bearing fruit in my life!

Listen & Obey

Spend two minutes in silent, listening prayer. Write down what you sense the Holy Spirit speaking to you.

Here are some prompts to assist you:

Lord, what do You want to speak to me from these passages of Scripture as I start my day?

How do You want me to apply this truth today?

Who do I need to share this with today?
