Before Day 1

Orientation

Vision

My college experience started with an orientation where leaders from the University took time to show incoming students around the campus and made sure we knew where our classes, the cafeteria, the bookstore, and the gym all were. They also gave us a campus map in case we got lost. The orientation was designed to help us get started on a good foot and get the most out of my freshman year.

As you get started on 40 Days of Discipleship, my hope is that this post serves as an orientation for you on this discipleship experience.

What to Expect

Each morning at 3 am from June 2nd to July 11th you will receive an email in your inbox that links to that day’s devotion. Each devotional in the 40-day series is designed to help you grow as a lifelong, obedient disciple of Jesus, who in turn helps others to do the same.

In addition to the daily email, you will be able to access the devotionals here: https://www.40dayjourneys.com/discipleship

FAQ

I love these devotionals, do you have more of them?
We are so glad you love the devotionals! In addition to 40 Days of Discipleship, we also have 40 Days of Devotion that you can sign up for as well. You can sign up for those here.

Can I share these with a friend?
Yes, please share them with whoever you think would benefit from them! Here is the link they can sign up with.

I signed up but am not getting the emails. What should I do?
This could happen for a number of reasons. Here are some things to check:

  • Are they going to your spam or trash folder?
  • Did you perhaps make a typo in your email address when you signed up?
  • If neither of these solve the problem, email the church and we will help you.
  • If all else fails, you can go straight to the devotionals using this link
