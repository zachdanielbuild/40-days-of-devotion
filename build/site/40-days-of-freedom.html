A 40 Day Journey

40 Days of Freedom

Designed to help you grow in the freedom and healing that Jesus died to give you.

We believe freedom and healing are for all God’s people and learning to walk in them is a key part of the abundant life Jesus desires for all of us.

Journey to Freedom

Our Journey To Freedom teaching series from the Fall of 2024 provides inspiration and practical, biblical foundations for walking in freedom and healing in Jesus. We encourage you to watch or listen to each one.

Pt 1 · You Can Be Free

In this sermon introducing the "Journey to Freedom" sermon series, Pastor Jimmy Seibert emphasizes that true freedom from sin is not only possible, but biblically promised through Christ's sacrifice and the Holy Spirit's power. He explores the process of sanctification, involving spirit, soul, and body, while emphasizing the crucial role of surrendering our will to God and the importance of community in our journey to freedom.

Pt 2 · Healing The Brokenhearted

The journey to freedom is a love story — one where God's unconditional love for us drives out fear, anxiety and brokenness. In this sermon, Jimmy Seibert teaches on how to work out our salvation, heal our broken hearts and find true freedom in Christ.

Pt 3 · The Framework of Freedom

In this message, Pastor Jimmy explored the process of sanctification and the renewal of our minds — key elements in experiencing the fullness of God’s love and purpose for our lives.

Pt 4 · Freedom From Idolatry

In this message, Pastor Mick explored the concept of idolatry and our journey to freedom from it. He emphasized how idolatry arises from our fears and insecurities, and how redirecting our worship to God is key to experiencing true freedom.

Pt 5 · Freedom From Bitterness

In this sermon, Pastor Jason Ramos explores the challenging topic of freedom from bitterness. Drawing from personal experiences and Scripture, he guides us through the process of identifying unforgiveness in our lives and embracing the transformative power of God’s grace.

Pt 6 · Freedom From Rebellion

Pastor Zach contrasts modern culture's view of freedom with Jesus's vision of true freedom. While our culture’s view of freedom leads to bondage, submission to Jesus leads to true and lasting freedom and and joy.

Pt 7 · Freedom From Pride

Through personal stories and Biblical wisdom, Joe Ewen illustrates how pride can imprison us while humility opens the door to God’s transformative grace. The sermon challenges us to examine our hearts, recognize areas of pride — both obvious and subtle — and embrace the freedom that comes through practicing genuine humility.

Pt 8 · Freedom From Lies

In this powerful message about freedom from lies and deception, Pastor Vincent Carpenter explores how deception can subtly influence our lives and relationships. Through Biblical examples and personal stories, he reveals how identifying lies and embracing God’s truth can lead to genuine freedom.

Pt 9 · The Freedom of Walking In The Spirit

The sermon explores the biblical concept of walking in the freedom of the Spirit. The speaker unpacks the contrast between the "flesh" and the "Spirit" and how we can align our minds, yield our will, and bring our emotions to God in order to experience the fruit of the Spirit - love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, faithfulness, gentleness, and self-control.

Pt 10 · Freedom From Generational Bondage

Understanding the impact of generational patterns — both blessings and curses — is vital for our spiritual growth and family legacy. In this powerful message, Pastor Jimmy Seibert explores how God's design for blessing can overcome generational struggles through Jesus Christ.
