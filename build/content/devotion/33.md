## Daily Devotional

As we close the Lord’s Prayer, we end with the declaration of worship that the Kingdom, the power, and the glory belong to the Lord, both now and forever. This is the anthem of heaven. It is not just a future reality, but one we step into right now when we come before the Lord in prayer. One day, this will be our constant song alongside believers or the ‘saints’ from every nation, tribe, and tongue (Revelation 7:9–12).

When we spend daily devotional time with God, we are doing more than checking off a spiritual discipline checklist. We are entering into that heavenly reality in the present. We are joining the saints across generations in worship and fellowship with Him. On this side of heaven, we practice this through daily devotion, affection and abiding in Him. What we are building now is a rhythm of daily life with God that we will carry with us into eternity.

## Prayer

We will continue to use The Lord’s Prayer as a framework for prayer and intercession today.

**Today’s focus is the SAINTS.**

Pray that God's name would be glorified in the lives of believers around the world. Ask that His Kingdom would come with fresh power in the global Church and that His will would be done in the lives of those who bear witness to His name. Intercede for saints who are suffering, facing persecution or experiencing weariness, that they would be renewed and strengthened in Christ. Pray for unity among believers across denominations and cultures, and ask God to protect His people from deception, division and spiritual attack. Ask that the global Church would rise up in this hour as a powerful witness to His Kingdom come.

## Listen & Obey

Spend two minutes in silent, listening prayer. Write down what you sense the Holy Spirit speaking to you. Here are some prompts to assist you:

- *Lord, what do You want to speak to me from these passages of Scripture as I start my day?*
- *How do You want me to apply this truth today?*
- *Who do I need to share this with today?*
