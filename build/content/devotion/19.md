## Word

> *Psalm 119:103 “How sweet are your words to my taste, sweeter than honey to my mouth!”*

## Daily Devotional

King David described God's Word as sweet like honey; Peter compared it to milk that satisfies a nursing baby. These metaphors teach us that God's Word nourishes our souls just as food nourishes our bodies. Just as we need food each day to strengthen our outer man, we need God's Word and to abide in Jesus to strengthen our inner man. And, because the Word of God is ‘living and active’, there is nothing else that can bring healing, comfort or guidance like it (Hebrews 4:12).

## Scriptures For Today

*Matthew 7:28-29 When Jesus had finished saying these things, the crowds were amazed at his teaching, because he taught as one who had authority, and not as their teachers of the law.*

*Proverbs 30:5 “Every word of God is flawless; he is a shield to those who take refuge in him.”*

*Isaiah 40:8 “The grass withers and the flowers fall, but the word of our God endures forever.”*

*John 17:17 “Sanctify them by the truth; your word is truth.”*

## Prayer

*Lord, Your Word is sweet to my taste and nourishing to my soul. Thank You for Your faithfulness to feed me Your Word, bringing me healing, comfort and guidance through it. Help me to be faithful to feast on this precious provision each day and not take Your Word for granted!*

## Listen & Obey

Spend two minutes in silent, listening prayer. Write down what you sense the Holy Spirit speaking to you. Here are some prompts to assist you:

- *Lord, what do You want to speak to me from these passages of Scripture as I start my day?*
- *How do You want me to apply this truth today?*
- *Who do I need to share this with today?*
