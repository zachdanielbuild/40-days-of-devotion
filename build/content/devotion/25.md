## Daily Devotional

From intimacy and adoration, Jesus leads us into intercession. The phrase “Your Kingdom come” is both an expression of our heart’s longing and a strategic prayer in the battlefields of this world. In today’s Scripture meditations, we see several snapshots of what it looks like for God’s Kingdom to come on earth as it is in heaven.

## Scriptures For Today

As you read today's Scriptures, read slowly and attentively, allowing the Holy Spirit to speak to you as you meditate on His Word.

## Prayer

We will continue to use the Lord’s Prayer as a framework for prayer and intercession today.

**Today’s focus is your EXTENDED FAMILY.**

Pray that God's name would be known and honored throughout your extended family. Ask that His Kingdom would come and will be done in their homes, their families and their lives. Pray for any family members that do not yet know Christ, that their hearts would be softened to the gospel. Pray that His will would replace any strongholds passed down through generations, and would be oriented to God’s will. Pray for provision, health, reconciliation and unity, and ask God for generations of your family to honor and glorify Him.

## Listen & Obey

Spend two minutes in silent, listening prayer. Write down what you sense the Holy Spirit speaking to you. Here are some prompts to assist you:

- *Lord, what do You want to speak to me from these passages of Scripture as I start my day?*
- *How do You want me to apply this truth today?*
- *Who do I need to share this with today?*
