## Daily Devotional

So far in our 40 Days of Devotion, we’ve seen that time in the Word, in worship and prayer are all essential building blocks to cultivate a life-giving devotional life. Our last section will add a final building block to our devotional time: listening.

Days 34–40 will focus on integrating the spiritual practice of listening. We will practice this discipline by establishing a daily rhythm of silence, focus, attentiveness, and surrender so that we may learn to recognize the voice of God and be transformed by His words, His love, and His counsel for us.

Scripture says the Holy Spirit is our Advocate, Counselor, Helper, Intercessor, Strengthener, Teacher, Reminder and Guide (John 14:16, 26, Romans 8:26). As we read, worship, pray and listen, we start our day by receiving His ministry to us and preparing our hearts to walk with Him throughout the day.

## Prayer

## Listen & Obey

Spend two minutes in silent, listening prayer. Write down what you sense the Holy Spirit speaking to you. Here are some prompts to assist you:

- *Lord, what do You want to speak to me from these passages of Scripture as I start my day?*
- *How do You want me to apply this truth today?*
- *Who do I need to share this with today?*
