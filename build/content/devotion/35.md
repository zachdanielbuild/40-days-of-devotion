## Daily Devotional

When we take time to listen, we choose to quiet our soul and be still before the Lord. Scripture teaches us that God speaks in many ways (Hebrews 1:1). One of those ways that we see often in Scripture, is that He speaks in the midst of silence and stillness. He spoke to Moses in the wilderness, David on the lonely hills of Bethlehem while he was tending sheep and to Elijah in the silence of the mountain cave. When we take time to be still before the Lord, we position ourselves to hear His voice.

## Prayer

*Lord, I sit in silence before you today and know that You are God. You will be exalted among the nations. Here in the quiet I listen for Your voice to speak to me.*

## Listen & Obey

Spend two minutes in silent, listening prayer. Write down what you sense the Holy Spirit speaking to you. Here are some prompts to assist you:

- *Lord, what do You want to speak to me from these passages of Scripture as I start my day?*
- *How do You want me to apply this truth today?*
- *Who do I need to share this with today?*
