## Word

> *Matthew 6:12a “and forgive us our debts…”*

## Daily Devotional

To pray this next line of the Lord’s Prayer with authenticity means to humbly acknowledge our sin and admit our need for God’s forgiveness. It’s a reminder that there is only one who had been without sin (2 Corinthians 5:21), and that without the sacrifice of Jesus, we would be owed eternal death apart from God. His forgiveness wipes our slates clean and releases us from our debts to walk forward in full freedom and grace.

## Prayer

We will continue to use the Lord’s Prayer as a framework for prayer and intercession today.

**Today’s focus is your CITY.**

Pray that God's name would be hallowed throughout your city, in its neighborhoods, businesses, institutions and people. Ask that His Kingdom would come and His will would be done in the decisions of local government, in schools, and in people of influence. Intercede for the poor, the marginalized and the lost within your city, that they would encounter God's love and provision. Pray against spiritual darkness and every evil thing, and ask God to raise up a Church in your city that is unified, bold and filled with the Spirit. Ask Him to revive and reform your city for His glory.

## Listen & Obey

Spend two minutes in silent, listening prayer. Write down what you sense the Holy Spirit speaking to you. Here are some prompts to assist you:

- *Lord, what do You want to speak to me from these passages of Scripture as I start my day?*
- *How do You want me to apply this truth today?*
- *Who do I need to share this with today?*
