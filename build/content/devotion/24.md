## Word

> *Matthew 6:9c “*hallowed be your name…”

## Daily Devotional

This first words of the Lord’s prayer center on intimacy. We are communing with our Father.

The second words express our breath-taking awe and wonder at the majestic splendor of who God is -”*Hallowed be Your Name!”*

He is:

- Jehovah-Jireh – Our Provider (Genesis 22:14)
- Jehovah-Rophe – Our Healer (Exodus 15:26)
- Jehovah-Nissi – Our Banner (Exodus 17:15)
- Jehovah-M’Kaddesh – The One Who Sanctifies Us (Exodus 31:13)
- Jehovah-Shalom – Our Peace (Judges 6:24)
- Jehovah-Rohi – Our Shepherd (Psalm 23:1)
- Jehovah-Tsidkenu – Our Righteousness (Jeremiah 23:6)
- Jehovah-Shammah – The One Who is There (Ezekiel 48:35)!

## Prayer

We will continue to use the Lord’s Prayer as a framework for prayer and intercession today.

**Today’s focus is your FAMILY or HOUSEHOLD.**

Pray for your family to be strengthened in Christ and honor God with their lives. Ask that His Kingdom would come and His will would be done in your family (spouse, kids, parents or roommates). Intercede for your family members, that they’d align their minds and hearts with the truth of Scripture. Pray for the overall welfare of your family, that righteousness, peace, provision, healing and spiritual renewal would mark their lives. Pray for protection over them and against any spiritual attack from the enemy.

## Listen & Obey

Spend two minutes in silent, listening prayer. Write down what you sense the Holy Spirit speaking to you. Here are some prompts to assist you:

- *Lord, what do You want to speak to me from these passages of Scripture as I start my day?*
- *How do You want me to apply this truth today?*
- *Who do I need to share this with today?*
