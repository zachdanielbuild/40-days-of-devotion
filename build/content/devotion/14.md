## Word

> *“...and you will know the truth, and the truth will make you free.” John 8:32*

## Daily Devotional

Another way we worship God is by declaring truth—about who He is, His character, and who He says we are. Declarations align our hearts and minds with what Scripture says is true, even when our feelings suggest otherwise. As we speak truth, our minds are renewed and our focus is reoriented to what is good and true (Philippians 4:8).

## Prayer

Today, write out two or three declarations rooted in Scripture that proclaim who God is and who He says you are. Then pray them out loud, agreeing with the Word of God and allowing these truths to renew your mind.

*Example:*

*God, You are good (Psalm 107:1).*

*God, I am a new creation (2 Corinthians 5:17).*

## Listen & Obey

Spend two minutes in silent, listening prayer. Write down what you sense the Holy Spirit speaking to you. Here are some prompts to assist you:

- *Lord, what do You want to speak to me from these passages of Scripture as I start my day?*
- *How do You want me to apply this truth today?*
- *Who do I need to share this with today?*
