## Word

> *Matthew 6:10c “On Earth as it is in Heaven…”*

## Daily Devotional

As Jesus completes this sentence, He gives us our target: that our desires would align with His will and that His Kingdom would come in every sphere of society. We remember that this world is not our final home and that our ultimate hope is in eternity with Him. Yet while we are here, our hearts ache for Him to bring heaven to earth, to make right what is broken and restore what has been lost.

It is easy for our prayer lives to become centered on our own wants or needs, but Jesus models something greater. He shows us the joy of interceding for those around Him and longing for the Father’s will to be done on earth as it is in heaven. Prayer becomes the vehicle through which we partner with God to see His will done.

## Prayer

We will continue to use the Lord’s Prayer as a framework for prayer and intercession today.

**Today’s focus is your LOCAL CHURCH.**

Pray that God’s name would be hallowed in and through your local church, in its leaders, families and witness within the community. Ask that His Kingdom would come and His will would be done in your church, through its gatherings and its ministry to people. Pray for unity, provision, protection and boldness. Ask God to supply everything the church needs to fulfill its calling and to guard it from division and every scheme of the enemy.

## Listen & Obey

Spend two minutes in silent, listening prayer. Write down what you sense the Holy Spirit speaking to you. Here are some prompts to assist you:

- *Lord, what do You want to speak to me from these passages of Scripture as I start my day?*
- *How do You want me to apply this truth today?*
- *Who do I need to share this with today?*
